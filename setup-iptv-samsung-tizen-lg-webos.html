Setup IPTV on Samsung Tizen & LG webOS Smart TVs – IPLAYTV

📅 Published: September 12, 2026 ✍️ Author: IPLAYTV ⏱️ 5 Min Read

How to Setup IPTV on Smart TV with Samsung Tizen & LG webOS

IPLAYTV makes it easy to turn any modern Samsung or LG smart TV into a powerful IPTV hub. Whether you own a Samsung Tizen TV or an LG webOS model, the process is similar – you just need the right app, a stable internet connection, and a few minutes of patience.

What You’ll Need

  • A Samsung TV running Tizen (2016 or newer) or an LG TV running webOS (2015 or newer).
  • A reliable broadband connection (minimum 5 Mbps for HD, 15 Mbps for 4K).
  • An active IPTV subscription from IPLAYTV (you’ll receive an M3U playlist URL and optional EPG link).
  • The IPLAYTV Player app – available in the Samsung Smart Hub and LG Content Store.
  • A remote control or a smartphone with the TV’s remote app for easier navigation.

Step‑by‑Step Installation on Samsung Tizen TVs

  1. Turn on your Samsung TV and press the Home button on the remote.
  2. Select Apps from the bottom menu.
  3. In the search bar, type IPLAYTV Player and press Enter.
  4. Click Install. The app will download and appear on your home screen.
  5. Open the app. You’ll be greeted with a welcome screen – choose Add Playlist.
  6. Enter the M3U URL provided by IPLAYTV. If you have an EPG (electronic program guide) URL, paste it in the optional field.
  7. Select Save. The app will load the channel list; this may take 30‑60 seconds.
  8. Navigate the channel list with your remote, select a channel, and enjoy streaming.

Step‑by‑Step Installation on LG webOS TVs

  1. Press the Home button on your LG remote and open the LG Content Store.
  2. Search for IPLAYTV Player using the on‑screen keyboard.
  3. Click Install. Once installed, launch the app from the home screen.
  4. On the first launch, tap + Add Playlist at the bottom of the screen.
  5. Paste the M3U URL from your IPLAYTV account. Add the EPG URL if you have one.
  6. Tap Confirm. The app will parse the playlist – this can take up to a minute for large lists.
  7. Use the arrow keys to scroll through categories, then press OK to start a channel.

Optimising Playback Quality

Even with a fast connection, you may notice buffering if the TV’s internal decoder isn’t set correctly. Follow these tweaks:

  • Go to Settings > General > Network Settings and enable Wired Ethernet if you have a LAN cable – it’s more stable than Wi‑Fi.
  • In the IPLAYTV Player, open Preferences and set Video Buffer Size to Medium (or High for 4K streams).
  • Disable any TV picture‑enhancement features such as Motion Smoothing or Auto Brightness that can add processing delay.

Troubleshooting Common Issues

Issue: No channels appear after adding the playlist.

Solution: Verify that the M3U URL is correct and not blocked by a firewall. Test the URL on a PC browser – it should download a plain‑text file.

Issue: Channels freeze after a few minutes.

Solution: Increase the buffer size in the app’s settings, and make sure your router isn’t throttling streaming traffic. A simple router reboot often clears temporary congestion.

Issue: Audio is out of sync with video.

Solution: In the player, enable Audio Sync Adjustment and set a delay of 100‑200 ms. If the problem persists, try a different HDMI port.

Advanced Tips for Power Users

For those who want a more customised experience, consider these optional steps:

  • Channel Grouping: Use the Favorites feature inside IPLAYTV Player to create genre‑based groups (Sports, Movies, Kids). This reduces navigation time.
  • External Media Player: If your TV struggles with high‑bitrate streams, connect a cheap Android TV box or Amazon Fire Stick, install the same IPLAYTV Player, and use HDMI‑CEC to control it with your TV remote.
  • VPN Protection: Some ISPs throttle IPTV traffic. A router‑level VPN (e.g., NordVPN) can hide the traffic and improve stability.

Conclusion

Setting up IPTV on Samsung Tizen or LG webOS TVs is straightforward when you use the dedicated IPLAYTV Player. By following the steps above, adjusting a few settings, and applying the troubleshooting tips, you’ll enjoy a seamless, cable‑free viewing experience in minutes.

FAQ

Do I need a separate IPTV box for Samsung or LG TVs?
No. The IPLAYTV Player runs natively on both Tizen and webOS, turning the TV itself into the streaming device.
Can I watch 4K channels on an older TV model?
Only if the TV’s hardware supports 4K playback. Older models will downscale the stream to the maximum resolution they can handle.
Is the IPLAYTV Player free?
The app is free to download, but you need an active IPLAYTV subscription to access channel playlists.
Will using a VPN affect my IPTV quality?
A high‑quality VPN with fast servers usually has minimal impact, but a slow VPN can add latency. Choose a server close to your location.
